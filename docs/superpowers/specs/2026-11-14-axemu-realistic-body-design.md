# AxEMU astronaut: realistic body and suit detail

Status: approved by the user 2026-11-14 (including builder-check rulings). Follows and narrows `2026-11-14-axemu-astronaut-design.md`; everything there about rig contract, actions, IK, camera and protected work still applies unless changed below.

## Intent and why

The user says the astronaut's **body shape** and **suit details** look the most fake. The current mesh is generated entirely from code primitives in `tools/blender/build_axemu.py` (lathed tubes, voxel-welded coverall, sphere gloves, box boots), and further tuning of that approach cannot reach a believable person-in-a-suit. Goal: a figure that reads as a real adult wearing the AxEMU lunar suit used for Artemis III/IV, credible at the gameplay camera's closest zoom (3 m; default 7 m, max 25 m). Audience is kids 7–12, so believability and readable silhouette matter more than engineering accuracy.

## Goals

- Anatomically proportioned wearer underneath; suit bulk added on top of real proportions (shoulder width, limb lengths, hand/foot size, neck/head placement).
- AxEMU details matching the 2024 flight-design reveal: hard upper torso with rear-entry hatch and PLSS, bubble helmet on the HUT with lamps/cameras and lowered gold visor, shoulder/elbow/hip/knee/wrist/ankle bearing rings with soft fabric between them, multi-layer gloves with distinct fingers, thick-soled lunar boots, chest display/controls, straps, pockets, sewn seams and reinforced patches.
- Detail authored in a high-poly version and baked to normal/AO maps on a game mesh, so folds and stitching hold up at 3 m.
- Drop-in replacement: same runtime contract, so walk, sample collection and module installation keep working without gameplay code changes beyond retuned constants.

## Non-goals

- **Motion stays as it is now** (user decision). No new or re-authored clips, no mocap, no gait redesign. Only rebinding, re-measuring bone lengths and retuning constants so the existing motion fits the new body.
- No visible face (visor lowered), no facial rig, no cloth simulation, no character customiser.
- No changes to camera, movement speeds, physics capsule (1.9 m / r 0.35 m), actions/timings, save format, other models or scenes.

## Anti-goals (fail even if it technically works)

- Still looks like assembled primitives: tube limbs, sphere hands, box boots, uniform smooth surfaces.
- Joints collapse, pinch or tear visibly when walking, crouching for samples, or reaching for connectors.
- Presented as official NASA/Artemis IV hardware, or carrying NASA/Axiom/Prada logos or an invented "official" mission patch.
- Breaks existing tests' intent, the fallback astronaut, or noticeably drops frame rate.

## Constraints

- Static Vite + Babylon.js 9 site; model is a single skinned GLB loaded by the existing pipeline (`src/models/loadModel.ts`, manifest in `src/models/manifest.ts`).
- Reproducible build: the GLB must be regenerated by a checked-in script (`blender --background --python tools/blender/build_axemu.py`, or a successor). Hand edits that are not reproducible from the repo are not acceptable. Any source meshes (base human, high-poly sculpt) used by the script are committed under `tools/blender/` (or `tools/blender/assets/`) with their licence.
- Licences: CC0 or own work only. Base human from MakeHuman/MPFB (CC0 assets). No paid or IP-restricted marketplace models.
- Each reference image used is credited in `public/models/SOURCES.md` and DESIGN.md → Data, per AGENTS.md.
- Blender 5.2 is installed; MPFB is not yet installed. Blender MCP (`mcp-for-blender` 2.2.9) is used to inspect results during the build; it must be connected (Blender running, MCP server started).

## Approaches

- **A — Anatomical base mesh + suit built over it, high-poly detail baked to a game mesh. Chosen by the user.** Correct proportions, real detail, clean licensing, editable; most effort.
- B — Licensed existing AxEMU model. Rejected: only paid, IP-restricted models exist (CGTrader, RenderHub, Blender Market); would also need re-rigging.
- C — AI-generated model (`generate_3d`). Rejected: messy topology deforms badly at joints, baked-in lighting, poor fit for a character that crouches and reaches.

## Decided design

### Wearer and proportions
- Adult base body ~1.75–1.80 m from MPFB/MakeHuman, neutral build. Used as the fitting form for the suit and for skin-weight transfer; the body itself is hidden (not exported) except where nothing covers it (nothing should be visible — the visor is opaque gold).
- Suited height stays ~1.90 m (manifest range 1.85–1.95 m). Joint centres (shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle) come from the base body, not hand-placed constants.

### Suit (reference: Axiom/Prada flight design, Oct 2024; NASA AxEMU photos)
- Hard upper torso (rigid, parented to `spine`), rear-entry hatch outline, PLSS backpack with service panels/vents.
- Bubble helmet with lowered gold visor, helmet lamps and cameras (`lamp_L`, `lamp_R` keep their role as the front marker).
- Bearing rings at shoulder, elbow (if visible in reference), wrist, hip/waist, ankle — rigid, following their bone.
- Soft segments (upper arm, forearm, waist brief, thighs, shins) with authored folds that bunch on the inside of bends.
- Gloves: modelled palm, four fingers and thumb with knuckle padding; existing finger bones drive them.
- Boots: shaped sole with tread, toe cap, ankle cuff.
- Outer layer white/off-white with grey accent panels as in the flight design; restrained red identification band kept as a game choice (marked as such in SOURCES.md).
- Details: chest display/controller, waist connectors, sample pouch, straps, pocket flaps, seams and stitching, knee/elbow reinforcement.

### Detail and materials
- High-poly sculpt/model → bake normal + AO (+ optional curvature for wear) onto the game mesh.
- Game mesh roughly 30–45k triangles including equipment; one 2K texture set for the suit (base colour, normal, ORM) plus a small set for visor/helmet/equipment. Up to ~5 material groups.
- Target GLB under ~8 MB; report actual size rather than silently degrading quality.

### Rig and runtime contract (must be preserved)
- Same skeleton bone names and hierarchy as now (pelvis, spine, neck, clavicles, upperArm/foreArm/wrist, thigh/shin/ankle/toe, finger/fingerTip ×5 per side), ≤4 influences per vertex.
- All node names in `manifest.ts` (`astro_root`, `torso`, `helmet`, `visor`, `plss`, `lamp_L/R`, `glove_*`, `boot_*`, …), sockets (`toolSocket_L/R`, `samplePouch`, `chestController`, `waistConnectors`) and materials listed there stay present — or the manifest/tests are migrated deliberately in the same change.
- The four existing clips (`idle`, `walk`, `collectSample`, `installModule`) are carried over unchanged in timing and intent; they are re-targeted only as far as new joint positions require (in-place, no root translation).
- Skin weights transferred from the base body, then cleaned at the bearings so rigid rings stay rigid and fabric bends between them.

### Builder-check rulings (Claude's calls, user may override)
- **Mesh structure:** the soft fabric stays one welded skinned coverall running continuously from neck ring to ankles, including under the hard torso; the rigid hard upper torso, bearings, helmet and PLSS are layered over it as bone-parented rigid parts. This keeps the "no disconnected shoulders/hips" fix and the intent of the existing single-shell and arm/torso cross-section tests; those tests may be adapted to the new shell's name/dimensions but not loosened in intent.
- **Shoulder pivot and arm rest angle:** keep the current pivots (shoulder ±0.325 m, wrist ±0.38 m) and slight arm splay, because motion is frozen and the clips/IK only rotate about X. The suit's shoulder bearing sits on that pivot (as on a real pressure suit, where the scye bearing sets the arm's rotation centre); the wearer's anatomical body is fitted inside around it. Other joint centres (elbow, hip, knee, ankle) come from the base body only where they stay within ~1 cm of today's; larger shifts require re-measuring IK constants as described below.
- **Helmet mounting:** helmet, visor and lamps become rigid parts of the hard upper torso (parented to `spine`), as on the real AxEMU where the bubble cannot nod. The small neck counter-lean/nod in walk and sample clips therefore stops being visible; no clip data changes. The `neck` bone stays for compatibility.

### Fitting existing motion to the new body
- Re-measure bone rest lengths from the new asset; update the gait/IK constants (`src/player/gait.ts` BODY, `astronautRig.ts`) and tool socket offsets.
- Check sample crouch, connector reach, walk, jump/land and rover seating for clipping (pack vs. ground, hands through thighs, helmet vs. torso) and fix in the mesh/weights, not by changing the motion.

## Verification

- Existing asset/rig/gait tests pass (updated only where measured constants legitimately change); the cross-section arm/torso clearance test still holds.
- New asset checks: triangle count, texture sizes, GLB size, bone count/names, socket presence, ≤4 influences.
- Visual: Blender MCP `look` renders of front/side/back/three-quarter at rest and in key poses (walk contact, sample low pose, install reach); in-game check at 3 m and 7 m. Final artistic acceptance by the user. No video.
- Build passes; one frame-time comparison old vs. new in the same scene/camera.
