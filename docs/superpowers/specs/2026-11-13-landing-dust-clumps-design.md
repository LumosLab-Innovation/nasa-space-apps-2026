## Implementation choices
Start gently below 20 m, strengthen below 5 m in proportion to thrust. Reuse the existing post-contact engine envelope; after emission ends, surviving clumps travel outward and fade within about 2–3 seconds. Irregular alpha texture, varied dimensions, limited upward launch, no stationary uniform ring. Sample terrain at each emission point. Preserve foot/rover dust, flight controls, camera, terrain and Earth. No new downloaded assets or per-frame resource creation.
