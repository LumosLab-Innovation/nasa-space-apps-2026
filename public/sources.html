ARTEMIS IV: COMMON GROUND · Sources / Nguồn dữ liệu

Real data. Your engineering decisions.

This educational game combines NASA datasets with simplified resource rules. It is a Space Apps team project, not a NASA flight simulator or an official mission plan.

Địa hình và vị trí thiên thể lấy từ dữ liệu NASA; mức tiêu thụ tài nguyên là luật game đơn giản hóa. Người chơi tự quyết định cách chia điện và chấp nhận hệ quả.

Terrain → hills and shadows

NASA GSFC / LRO LOLA: high-resolution lunar south pole topography. Connecting Ridge, Site01. A 3 km crop is scaled to 1.5 km, with heights scaled equally to preserve slopes. Terrain shapes the route and blocks sunlight.

Exact product, citation and processing

Sun and Earth → night length and communications

NASA/JPL Horizons supplies hourly Sun/Earth azimuth and elevation for 2028 at 89.45° S, 222.7° E. Combined with the terrain, it determines the long shadow at camp (approximately 86 hours in the selected scenario). Predicting sunrise is part of your task.

Exact observation parameters and query links

Solar particles → storm context

NASA CCMC DONKI supplies recorded solar energetic particle events. The game tries the live service and falls back to a shipped snapshot. If both fail, it uses a generic explanation instead of inventing an event.

The countdown, protection zones and dose messages are teaching abstractions, not calibrated radiation predictions. Snapshot provenance.

What is simplified or fictional?

  • PIP, MICA and the story are fictional. The mission dates are a game scenario.
  • Night energy uses percentages per rounded night-day. Heater, freezer, robot warmers, lights and comms share one reserve. These are game balance values, not NASA spacecraft specifications.
  • Keeping MICA warm can cost a science log if the sample freezer is switched off. Sealing heat leaks reduces heater demand.
  • Rescue helps you continue learning. It is not a prediction of survival or a procedure for real astronauts.
  • The Moon lacks a substantial atmosphere for aerodynamic re-entry heating.
