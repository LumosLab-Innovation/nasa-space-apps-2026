# Earth texture

- `blue-marble-2048.jpg` (2048 × 1024) is NASA SVS's [equirectangular Blue Marble Next Generation visualization with clouds](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3615/), supplied as [flat_earth03.jpg](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a003600/a003615/flat_earth03.jpg).
- Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, NASA Earth Observatory, and Reto Stöckli. The visualization is by Greg Shirah and uses the Climate Prediction Center cloud composite.
- The game's Earth phase follows its scene lighting. The texture uses a static cloud composite. The opening shot enlarges Earth's disc 2.25×, and the atmospheric limb and halo are inspired by [Artemis II's Earthset photograph](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/earthset/).
